Pass `TalariaNavigatorObserver` in `MaterialApp.navigatorObservers` (and inside `TalariaScreenCapture` when you use heatmaps). That is the supported way to tag screens. See the [Flutter hub](/sdk/flutter/) for the wrap-once example.
